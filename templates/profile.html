⚙ How Personalisation Works

When you click a search result, the document is copied into a personal user_history Elasticsearch index. This index uses the same BM25 similarity settings as the main wiki index.

When you perform a new search with personalisation enabled, your query is run through BM25 on both the main index and your history index. The final score is a weighted blend:

final = (1 − α) × BM25main + α × BM25history

Documents that match your past interests (based on what you've clicked) will receive a boost and may be re-ranked higher. The α parameter controls how aggressively personalisation affects results.
